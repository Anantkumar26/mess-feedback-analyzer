# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students give feedback about mess food, but the feedback may not be organised. A simple system is needed to collect ratings and comments and show the overall rating.

## Scope
The project focuses on collecting basic food feedback and calculating an overall average rating.

## Target Users
- College students
- Mess committee
- Mess management

## High-Level Features
- Add dish feedback
- Give rating from 1 to 5
- Save feedback
- View feedback
- Calculate average rating
